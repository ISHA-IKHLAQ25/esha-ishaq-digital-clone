## My hot takes

1. **A degree does not guarantee skills.** Practical experience matters much more. A degree is a piece of paper; what you can actually build is what counts.
2. **Universities focus too heavily on grades** rather than real-world problem-solving. A high GPA does not mean you can solve a real client's problem.
3. **AI will not replace good programmers, but programmers who use AI will replace those who do not.**

## AI and new programmers

Is AI going to help or hurt new programmers? I think it depends on how you use it. AI is really beneficial when you use it as a tool: to explain a concept you did not understand, or to help you debug. But if you blindly copy code from AI without understanding it, you build a dangerous dependency, and the moment something breaks you are stuck. Core fundamentals are still essential. Use AI to learn faster, not to skip learning.

## Tools I like and dislike

**Preferred stack:** React, Node.js, Supabase, and VS Code as my editor.

**What I dislike:** complex tools and bloated, unnecessary setup procedures. If a tool needs an hour of configuration before you can write one line of useful code, that is a problem. I like tools that let me get to work quickly.

## What annoys me most
