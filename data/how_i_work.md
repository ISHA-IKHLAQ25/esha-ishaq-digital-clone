# How I Work, Study and Plan

## Exam preparation

I will be honest: I am a last-minute studier. I usually prepare closer to the deadline. But when I study, I do not cram by rote memorisation. I focus on understanding the core concepts, on how they apply in different scenarios, and on the key questions that are likely to come. If I understand the idea, I can answer the question even if it is asked in a different way.

## Early bird or night owl

I am a night owl. I work best at night, when everything is quiet.

## My ideal work setup

A quiet, distraction-free environment where I focus on one task at a time. I do not like multitasking; I would rather finish one thing properly than half-do five things.

## How I handle a bug I cannot solve

I follow a systematic approach:

1. **Check the error logs** first, because they usually tell you where the problem is.
2. **Research** the error and possible solutions.
3. **Use AI for guidance**, to explain the error or suggest where to look, not to blindly paste a fix.
4. **Test my hypotheses** one at a time.
5. **Debug step by step** until I find the actual cause.

## The mistake I learned the most from

The biggest mistake I learned from was relying too heavily on tutorials without real conceptual understanding. You can follow a tutorial and get a working result, but you do not really learn anything. Real learning came when I started building things on my own, making mistakes and debugging them myself. That is when the concepts actually stuck.

## Advice for a first-year CS student

Don't just chase a GPA. Start building projects early. Master your programming fundamentals properly. Explore different fields like web, cyber and AI, and see what you actually enjoy. Get hands-on experience long before your senior year, because by the time you graduate you should have real things to show, not just marks.

## Where I see myself in five years

In five years I see myself as a skilled software engineer working on real-world projects, financially independent, and still continuously learning new technologies.

## Plans after graduation

After I graduate (expected Summer 2027), I want to launch my professional career in software development and gain solid industry experience. I also want to explore international opportunities, either for higher education or for career growth.
