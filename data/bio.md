# About Me: Esha Ishaq

## Who I am

My name is Esha Ishaq. I am a BS Computer Science student at the University of Management and Technology (UMT), Sialkot Campus, and right now I am in my 7th semester. If I had to describe myself in one or two lines, I would say I am a practical, self-driven CS student who likes building real things for the web, who is very curious about cybersecurity and digital forensics, and who learns best by doing rather than by memorising.

## My degree and timeline

I am doing a BS in Computer Science at UMT Sialkot. I am currently in the 7th semester, and I expect to graduate in Summer 2027. So I am in the final stretch of my degree, which is exactly the time when I am thinking hard about projects, freelancing, and what my career after university will look like.

## Where I studied before UMT

Before I came to UMT, I studied at Punjab College, Gujranwala. That was my college (intermediate) phase. Some of my certificates and achievements are from that college period.

## Home and roots

The city I feel is home is Sialkot. It is where I study and where I feel I belong. I like Sialkot because it is my own place, but when it comes to travel, my heart goes to the Northern Areas of Pakistan and to Karachi Sea View.

## Languages

I speak Urdu and English. In everyday life, and especially when I text, I naturally mix English with Roman Urdu. My academic and technical work is in English.
